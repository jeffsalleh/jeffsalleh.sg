If you're reading this, you're on my new website.

For a while my personal site lived on Wix. It served its purpose — but I'd been wanting more control, faster pages, cleaner code, and honestly a lower recurring cost. So over a few evenings, I moved it.

The site you're on now is built with [Astro](https://astro.build), hosted on Cloudflare Pages, and deployed from a Git repository I own. Everything is static, fast, and mine. If you'd rather see the latest writing than read the meta story, [start here](/post/twenty-five-years-since-frankston).

## What's different

A few things that weren't possible on Wix — or were possible only clumsily:

- **A [proper About page](/about)** that tells you who I actually am — the arc from Monash to A*STAR to Oracle's Asia R&D Center to the Java Platform Group — rather than a resume.
- **Tag filtering** on the homepage: click `career`, `community`, `ai`, or any other tag to narrow the archive to that lens.
- **A "Featured" card** that surfaces whatever I've written most recently.
- **Real speed.** Static pages served from Cloudflare's global edge. It just feels faster.

Small things. But together they make it a place I actually want to write.

## The small experiment

Here's the part I want to be open about: I built this whole thing with **[Claude](https://claude.ai) by Anthropic** as my collaborator. Specifically, I used three of Anthropic's products in different roles:

- **Claude in chat** for architecture, design direction, editorial help, and thinking-out-loud partnership.
- **[Claude Code](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-code)** for the actual engineering — writing components, wrestling with a stubborn CSS bug at 11pm, generating the Wix migration scripts.
- **Claude Design (beta)** for the visual polish — the homepage layout, the About page structure, the little details that make it feel like a considered site rather than a template.

I wanted to see what it's like to actually ship a real project with these tools in the loop. Not a toy, not a demo — my personal website, published to my own domain, with my name on it. Two goals:

1. **Understand these tools deeply.** As someone leading engineering teams, I need a first-hand feel for how AI collaboration actually works in practice — the good, the awkward, and the surprising.
2. **Save some money.** No Wix subscription. Cloudflare Pages is free. The only cost now is my `.sg` domain renewal.

I'll write more about the experience in a future post — what worked well, what didn't, and what a senior engineer who still codes should take away from working this way.

## And the real point

The site is also a kickoff.

My [first post](/post/twenty-five-years-since-frankston) is a reflection on going back to my university campus twenty-five years after I graduated. Standing there, I left with a renewed sense of optimism — that whatever's next is more opportunity than obstacle.

It felt right to launch this new place with that piece.

Have a look around. And if you're an aspiring technologist reading this, especially early in your career — reach out. I'd genuinely like to hear from you.
