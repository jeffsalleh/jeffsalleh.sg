Working with Darul Ghufran over the last 3 weeks on how we can keep the community informed towards the official launch of the new mosque premise. With over 700+ contacts currently in Darul Ghufran's CRM system, we decided to keep them updated using EDMs on activities and happenings at Masjid Darul Ghufran especially information about the official launch.

Now that we are pretty set in the setting up CiviMail and the experience we want from the Darul Ghufran's congregation, I look forward to see how this feature can bring the community closer to the mosque and hoping to hear feedback from them. I will post a more technical posting on using CiviMail in another post.
