Last week was one of those weeks that couldn’t get any busier—QBR, key meetings, and then a four-day "training" that was anything but ordinary.

Earlier this year, I was nominated by **Yayasan Mendaki** to join the **Singapore Leadership Network (SGLN)**, a program managed by **Human Capital Leadership Institute (HCLI)**. At first, I hesitated. Given my schedule, carving out time felt like a stretch. But after reflecting and speaking with my manager and HR counterpart, I realized this was more than just another leadership program—it was an opportunity to **take flight**.

With a bold vision to **develop global-ready leaders**, SGLN is a nine-month journey designed to foster **deep connections, accelerate careers, and open doors beyond Singapore and the region**. Alongside **65 driven, ambitious professionals from diverse industries**, I embarked on this **fellowship of transformation**.

I know the months ahead will demand a lot from me, but in just four days, I have already **met, shared ideas, and laughed** with incredible individuals who I believe will be future global leaders. The energy is contagious. The possibilities are boundless. And I feel **ready to launch**.
  

🚀 _Ground Control to Major Tom_🚀 _Take your protein pills and put your helmet on_🚀 _Commencing countdown, engines on..._  

This is more than just a training—it's the ignition sequence for a **larger mission**. **We are accelerating, forging alliances, and taking our place on the global stage.**


Thank you, **Yayasan Mendaki**, for the nomination, and my deep appreciation to **Georges Saab and Oracle** for the support in making this journey possible.
  

**Check ignition and may God's love be with us.** 🔥
